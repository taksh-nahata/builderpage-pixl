Ventre

This is another hardware project I've built. This is a lot more complex. Basically this is like a "Stool Analyzer". How it works is when someone defecates, it captures data from their sitting such as stool size, texture, rate, consistency, etc. Using this information, it can analyze it and provide insights into their gut and overall health, including hydration, blood detection, overall gut health score, and more. We weren't able to finish this project because we realized it would be quite difficult to collect data for it.

TechSteps

This is one of the websites I've been working on. It's basically a platform for people to get the tech help they need easily in step-by-step flashcard form, and also communicate with people with similar problems. It is primarily made for older people, since technology is evolving so fast nowadays, as a way for them to keep up and figure out how to do something without having to call for help or just give up. I decided to create it after seeing the problems some older people were having when using computers at the library as well as the problems my own grandparents have everyday. It is currently in development.
